Design a logo for "NONOX", an open-source Swedish bookkeeping and accounting software.
Concept: freedom and openness. Open source forever, customers can take all data and leave at any time. Express openness and no lock-in.
Symbol: bold geometric vermilion #FF5A36 ring with a clean real gap cut into the right side, like an open circle. Must work alone at 32 px.
Wordmark: exact text "NONOX", five letters N O N O X. Heavy, wide geometric sans-serif capitals, tight even spacing. Both O letters echo the open ring with a small right-side gap. N and X solid simple.
Horizontal lockup, symbol left, wordmark right, vertically centered. Wordmark ink black #15171C; warm off-white #F7F5F0 background. Gaps are genuine negative space showing background, not closed colored bridges.
Flat vector appearance, crisp edges, modern confident Scandinavian finance brand. Generous empty space, centered canvas.
Avoid green, leaves, checkmarks, coins, piggy banks, calculators, ledger books, keyhole padlocks, swooshes, globes, gradients, shadows, 3D, textures, mockups, mascots, resemblance to existing accounting brands. No extra text.
